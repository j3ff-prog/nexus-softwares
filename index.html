About

A small team. A high standard.

Nexus Softwares was founded to give Kenyan businesses access to proper systems, not just software installations, but workflows that actually make sense for how the business operates. We work directly with clients, which means faster communication, faster delivery, and full accountability.

Our core work is Zoho Books consultancy: setting up accounts, configuring eTIMS integration for KRA compliance, building automation with Deluge, and training teams. We also build websites for clients who need a professional online presence.
